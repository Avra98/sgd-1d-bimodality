Factors generally do not commute when the misalignment \(\phi>0\). All plots are in a fixed lab frame \((x_1,x_2)\) — not a rotating eigenframe. The commutative 2-D lab is unchanged: sgd-2d-bimodality.html.

$$x_{t+1}=(I-\eta H_t)\,x_t-\eta\Big(\tfrac{c_4}{6}\|x_t\|^2+\tfrac{c_6}{120}\|x_t\|^4\Big)x_t+\sigma\,\xi_t, \qquad H_t=R(\theta_t)\,\mathrm{diag}(\lambda_{1,t},\lambda_{2,t})\,R(\theta_t)^\top$$
Stochasticity lives only in \(H_t\). Self-stabilization is a deterministic isotropic quartic/sextic in the lab frame (start mild — aggressive GIF \(c_4\) is often too strong here). Linear diagnostics use \(\prod(I-\eta H_t)\): top Lyapunov \(\chi\) and short-horizon \(\|M_k\|_2\).
